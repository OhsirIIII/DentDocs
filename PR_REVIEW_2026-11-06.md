## Findings resolved

| Priority | Finding and trigger | Resulting behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | A clinical version followed by two administrative revisions left the clinical text inside nested history. Front-desk filtering checked only the outer history entries, exposing the nested clinical entry through bootstrap/read/search responses. | Responses flatten legacy history before role filtering. Clinical history stays hidden from front-desk users; authorized staff retain the version and original-file access. Repeated versions recursively duplicated earlier history and could grow exponentially. | A shared snapshot helper excludes history before copying. The document retains one flat history index; newly written version snapshots contain only that version's fields. Legacy history is flattened on reads and subsequent writes/restores without a bulk migration. |
